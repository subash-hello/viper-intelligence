01

Scope & Engineering Philosophy

Viper Intelligence (“we”, “our”, or “us”), registered and operating in Kathmandu, Nepal under lead architects Subash Bhandari and Rohan Aacharya, provides bespoke high-performance software engineering, native mobile suites, cloud architecture, and artificial intelligence solutions.

We firmly believe that engineering integrity begins with absolute respect for client confidentiality and data ownership. This Privacy Policy outlines our transparent standards for collecting, processing, securing, and discarding data throughout discovery consultations, production sprints, and ongoing SLA maintenance.

Zero-Data Monetization Guarantee

We do not sell, rent, broker, or trade client contact details, project specifications, proprietary code, or usage telemetry to any third-party advertisers, data aggregators, or unauthorized external parties. Ever.

02

Information We Collect

We only collect information strictly required to engineer, evaluate, quote, and deliver your requested software solutions. Information collected is categorized into:

  • ◆
    Client Discovery Details: Name, professional email address (e.g. viperintelligence1@gmail.com communications), telephone / WhatsApp number, company name, and project scope submitted via consultation forms or WhatsApp.
  • ◆
    Project Technical Artifacts: Architecture diagrams, functional specifications, third-party API credentials provided for sandbox integration, Figma mockups, and database schemas.
  • ◆
    Essential Web Telemetry: Anonymized server logs including request timestamps, response latency, device viewport metrics, and browser user-agent headers strictly used to optimize Web Vitals and resolve rendering bottlenecks.

We do not deploy intrusive behavioral advertising pixels, tracking canvas fingerprinting, or third-party marketing beacons across our web platforms.

04

Source Code & Intellectual Property Protection

At Viper Intelligence, intellectual property integrity is our hallmark. Unlike legacy software agencies that retain rights to your codebase or lock you into proprietary platforms:

100% Full IP & Git Repository Handover

Upon milestone settlement, 100% of all custom-written source code, configuration scripts, deployment pipelines, database migrations, and design assets are legally transferred to your organization. Zero proprietary lock-in. Zero recurring agency royalty fees.

All proprietary algorithms, client business logic, and custom training datasets are treated as strictly confidential under binding bilateral NDA standards. We never train public machine learning models on your private enterprise data.

08

Security Architecture & Encryption Standards

Security is not an afterthought at Viper Intelligence; it is our primary architectural principle:

  • ◆
    In-Transit Encryption: Enforced TLS 1.3 encryption across all public interfaces with strict HSTS (HTTP Strict Transport Security) headers.
  • ◆
    At-Rest Encryption: All engineering workspaces, production keys, and database volumes are encrypted using AES-256-GCM standards.
  • ◆
    Zero-Trust Access Control: Two-factor authentication (2FA) and biometric hardware keys are enforced for all production server and repository access.
  • ◆
    Incident Protocol: In the unlikely event of any security vulnerability affecting client assets, we commit to notifying affected clients within 24 hours with remediation telemetry.
